AR Solar System Explorer

An interactive browser-based augmented reality experience for exploring the Sun, all eight planets, the Moon, and a floating astronaut — anchored either to a printed image marker or directly onto the real surfaces around you.

1

🎯 Marker-Based AR

Scan your trained image target with MindAR to anchor the solar system in place above it. Cycle through three real orbital animations, pinch to resize, drag to spin, and send the astronaut hopping between waypoints with a spin trick of its own.

🌌 Markerless AR

Tap "Start AR", scan any flat surface and place the whole system in your room. The astronaut patrols the orbits and collides with passing planets, a live satellite dot tracks the real ISS from an open API, and tapping any body opens an info card. Pinch to scale, drag to rotate.

Before you start

  • Marker AR works in any recent mobile or desktop browser with camera access — have your trained image target ready to print or display on a second screen.
  • Markerless AR needs a WebXR-capable browser with AR support (e.g. Chrome on Android/ARCore). It is not currently available in desktop browsers or iOS Safari.
  • Use good, even lighting and move your device slowly while the app looks for a marker or a surface.
  • Tap anywhere once to enable audio — browsers block sound until you interact with the page.
